# Data notes

What the real IBB data looked like when it was built on 2026-11-15. Later tasks rely on these facts.

## Sources

- Hourly ridership: IBB Open Data Portal, dataset `hourly-public-transport-data-set` (https://data.ibb.gov.tr/dataset/hourly-public-transport-data-set)
- Monthly trips per rail line: dataset `rayli-sistemler-hat-bazli-sefer-sayilari` (https://data.ibb.gov.tr/dataset/rayli-sistemler-hat-bazli-sefer-sayilari)
- Calendar (`data/calendar.csv`, generated by `scripts/make_calendar.py`). Dates were cross-checked on news and reference sites that quote the official calendars; the Ministry of Education circulars and Resmî Gazete themselves were not opened:
  - School calendar 2022-23: https://www.turkedebiyati.org/2022-2023-meb-calisma-is-gunu-takvimi/ and https://arac.meb.gov.tr/meb_iys_dosyalar/2023_06/01085828_09121918_2022-2023_CYalYsYma_Takvimi.pdf (semester break extended to 20 Feb 2023 after the earthquake)
  - School calendar 2023-24: https://kirklareli.meb.gov.tr/meb_iys_dosyalar/2023_08/18101216_2023-2024_CalYYma_Takvimi.pdf
  - School calendar 2024-25: https://www.ntv.com.tr/egitim/2024-2025-egitim-ogretim-yili-takvimi-belli-oldu-okullar-ne-zaman-acilacak-ara-tatil-tarihleri-ne-zaman,qb0M0SGv6EGrQXtgh92TuQ
  - Public and religious holidays 2023: https://cottgroup.com/en/legislation/item/2023-resmi-tatil-gunleri-ve-mesai-hesaplama ; 2024: https://cottgroup.com/en/blog/work-life/item/2024-resmi-tatil-gunleri-ve-mesai-hesaplama
  - Summer break is entered as the weekdays between the last school day and the first school day. Administrative-leave days around the bayrams are not marked separately. The half-day on 28 October is not marked.

## The data is incomplete from August 2024 (most important finding)

January 2023 to July 2024 (19 months, 577 days) has every day complete. From August 2024 most days are nearly empty in the source files themselves, for buses and ferries as well as rail:

| Month | Days in file | Complete days | Which |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024-08 | 31 | 6 | 1, 25, 27, 28, 30, 31 |
| 2024-09 | 30 | 8 | 1, 4–10 |
| 2024-10 | 18 (file ends on the 18th) | 5 | 2, 4, 16, 17, 18 |

A complete day has 1.7–3.5 million rail boardings; the other days have at most 7,695. This, not a change in row detail, is why the files shrink from about 1.7 GB to about 0.3 GB.

On the 19 complete days the numbers continue smoothly from July 2024. Weekday average boardings per line, complete days only, relative to July 2024: August 0.86–0.95 for the large lines (M1 0.90, M2 0.86, M4 0.86, M5 0.90, M7 0.91, T1 0.95, Marmaray 0.93), September 0.95–1.03, October 1.07–1.24. At station level the August/July ratio has median 0.90, with 90% of units between 0.77 and 1.01. Ticket-type and transfer shares are unchanged across the break (full fare 44.7% → 46.6%, discounted 44.3% → 41.9%, free 10.6% → 11.0%, transfers 13.2% → 13.5%). Based on 4–5 complete weekdays per month, so the ratios are rough.

Consequence: monthly totals for August–October 2024 are not usable, and the planned backtest window (September–October 2024) has only 13 complete days.

## Portal quirks

- The resource titled "Aralık 2024" is the file `hourly_transportation_202512.csv`; it and November 2024 are header-only. `build_data.py` matches on the file name and checks every date against the expected month.
- The server ignores range requests and often drops large transfers; downloads restart from zero.
- 2023-01..2024-10 is 31.5 GB of CSV; the rail aggregate is 35 MB.

## Lines

24 `line_name` values under `road_type = 'RAYLI'`. All 17 lines of the trips file are present for all 22 months, and the trips file has all 17 for every month from 2023-01 to 2024-11 (it runs 2017-01 to 2025-12).

- With trips data (recommendation card possible): M1 M2 M3 M4 M5 M6 M7 M8 M9 T1 T3 T4 T5 F1 F4 TF1 TF2.
- Demand view only: MARMARAY (the largest single line, 19.7% of rail boardings), TCDD-GH, TCDD-SK (starts 2024-02), TCDD-HB, TUNEL, NOSTRAM.
- `34A`: 36 boardings on one day (2023-08-11); a stray code to drop.

Share of rail boardings: Marmaray 19.7%, M2 15.1%, T1 12.3%, M1 11.6%, M4 9.8%, M5 9.0%, M7 6.9%, T4 6.0%, the rest under 3% each.

## Stations

- `station_poi_desc_cd` is often an entrance, not a station: e.g. `SISLI 2 KUZEY`, `YENIKAPI KUZEY`, `MAHMUTBEY M7 HOL 1`. There are 384 line-station units up to July 2024.
- 24 names appear under more than one line (Yenikapı, Aksaray, Kozyatağı, …). These are separate gate lines per line, so line-station pairs stay the unit.
- Names are plain ASCII capitals; no normalization map was needed. Some stations were renamed or re-coded mid-period, especially on M9 (`IHLAS YUVA` → `M9 - 29 EKIM CUMHURIYET - IHLAS YUVA` → `M9 29 EKIM CUMHURIYET-IHLAS YUVA`; `M9 IKITELLI` ends 2024-02).
- Blank station: T3 (1.9 M boardings) and NOSTRAM (0.9 M) have no station name for 19 of the months; they become `UNKNOWN`, i.e. one unit per line. Elsewhere blanks are negligible (under 1,300 boardings).
- 63 units have fewer than 12 months of data up to July 2024 (1.75% of boardings): the 2024 openings and extensions on M3, M4, M5, M8, M9, T4, TCDD-GH and all of TCDD-SK. They are demand-view only.

## Ticket types (July 2024, rail)

`TAM` 44.7%, `INDIRIMLI1` 41.5%, `INDIRIMLI2` 2.8%, `UCRETSIZ` 10.6%, `PERSONEL` 0.3%, blank 0.1%, plus `DENETIM`, `GIRIS_CIKIS`, `KULLANILMAYAN_GRUBU` near zero. No value means "student" by itself: `INDIRIMLI1` at 41.5% is too large to be students only. The feature stays "discounted share" (`INDIRIMLI%`). Transfers are 13.2% of boardings.

## Hours

Hours 0–5 carry 0.69% of rail boardings.
